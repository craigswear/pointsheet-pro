How It Works

Up and running in 5 minutes

1

Add Your Students

Create student profiles, set grade level, teacher name, and goal percentage targets.

2

Define Behavioral Goals

Enter 3–8 goals per student. Use existing IEP goals or create your own PBIS targets.

3

Track Points by Period

Click 0, 1, or 2 for each goal each period. Sheet auto-calculates totals and daily percentage.

4

Share & Report

Email parents a daily summary, export a weekly report, or print for IEP binders.
