Why PointSheet Pro

Everything paper does — and so much more

Designed with special education teachers, behavior interventionists, and school psychologists.

📋

Customizable Goals

Set up any behavioral goals per student — stay on task, respectful language, follow directions, or any IEP-specific target. Up to 8 goals per sheet.

📊

Real-Time Tracking

Daily, weekly, and monthly trends for every student. Automatic percentage calculations. Color-coded indicators at a glance. Saved to Firebase instantly.

📤

Parent & Team Sharing

Send daily score summaries to parents via email or PDF. Share with case managers, behavioral specialists, and paraprofessionals.

🖨

Print-Perfect Layout

Every point sheet prints beautifully on a single page. Use digital OR paper — works both ways. Great for IEP documentation.

📈

IEP-Ready Reports

Generate progress reports with charts and trend data. Export to PDF for IEP meetings, parent conferences, and behavior reviews.

⚡

Any Device

Chromebooks, iPads, phones, or computers. No download needed. Teachers and paras access the same sheet simultaneously in real time.

How It Works

Up and running in 5 minutes

1

Create Your Account

Sign up free with email or Google. Your data is securely stored in Firebase.

2

Add Students & Goals

Create student profiles and enter their behavioral goals — IEP targets or your own PBIS goals.

3

Track Points by Period

Click 0, 1, or 2 for each goal each period. Totals and percentages auto-calculate and save.

4

Share & Report

Email parents a daily summary, export a weekly report, or print for IEP binders.
